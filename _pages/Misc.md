#### About my name

**Hang (航)** is the given name from my parents. *Hang* means "to navigate" or "to sail" in Chinese, symbolizing exploration, direction, and moving forward with purpose. It reflects the idea of venturing into the unknown with determination. It can be pronounced as *"hung"* (similar to *Hong*, with a flat tone).

**Guo (郭)** is the family name from my ancestors, a well-established Chinese surname with a long history. It can be pronounced as *"gwor"*, similar to *"gore"* but softer.
